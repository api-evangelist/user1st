# User1st

> User1st is a web and mobile digital accessibility company. Its U1 Suite helps organizations reach and maintain WCAG, ADA, and European Accessibility Act (EAA) compliance across web frameworks (JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Angular, Vue, WordPress, Shopify) and mobile platforms (iOS Native, Android Native, React Native, Flutter). Backed by 500 Global.
